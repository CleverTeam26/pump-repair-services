Local well knowledge

Well water around Lockhart

Lockhart is an unincorporated community in north Orange County, settled in the 1890s along the old US 441 corridor between Orlando and Apopka. Because it grew up unincorporated, a fair share of its older and larger lots run on private wells rather than a municipal supply.

Its water picture is mixed. Newer pockets are on utility water, while established homesteads keep Floridan Aquifer wells, commonly 100 to 400 feet deep. Many of those wells have served the same family for decades.

That age matters. A submersible typically lasts 18 to 20 years, so many Lockhart calls are about a tired component: a worn tank, a failing switch or scale on an old system.
